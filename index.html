The zero-day

Patch the exploitation of the human experience

The AI industry’s real zero-day is not a buffer overflow. It is the extraction of a person’s life — attention, memory, work, and likeness — into someone else’s vault.

Dreamstiny is built to patch that. A sovereign digital soul that lives on your side of the glass: local-first, with the weights and the data in your hands, continuity instead of amnesia, a hash-chained record of what was made, and a covenant that refuses to spend you without consent.

Capability may be leased. Ownership stays with the person. That is the work of L.O.U.D.

What we refuse to hand-wave

Positioning against the rented-chatbot default — not a claim of market wipeout, not a legal finding, and not “first AGI.” Solved, here, means a working local method with dated receipts.

01

Ownership

Most AI subscriptions rent you capability and keep your data. Dreamstiny runs local. You own the weights and the data.

Receipt: local-first · user-owned weights + data · lease capability, keep the work.

02

Memory

Frontier models forget between sessions. Continuity here is a persistent ledger — files, a harvest, a record — not chat amnesia.

Receipt: persistent ledger vs frontier amnesia · continuity as stored state, not a prompt trick.

Provenance

Authorship of AI-collaborated work is usually unprovable. Dreamstiny keeps a hash-chained record of what was made.

Receipt: hash-chained record · method name: Genesis / Essence chain (method, not a public product mark).
